?? Supercharged Quiz App Rulebook

1. Adaptive Difficulty (3D Array Bank):

Questions are indexed via Question[Category][Difficulty][Index]. 2 consecutive correct answers elevate difficulty (EASY ? MEDIUM ? HARD). 1 incorrect answer drops difficulty down.

2. Lifeline System:

50/50: Removes two incorrect options.
Skip Question: Advances without penalty.
Audience Hint: Gives a helpful clue.
Single-Use Guard: Overused lifelines throw custom LifelineAlreadyUsedException.

3. Time Attack & Speed Bonuses:

Answering in ≤ 5.0s awards +5 pts bonus. ≤ 10.0s awards +2 pts bonus.

4. Anti-Cheat System:

Responses submitted in < 2.0s trigger TooFastAnswerException and anti-cheat review flags.

5. HashMap Progress Tracking & File Export:

Scores are aggregated per category. Produces formatted summary report with Strongest & Weakest categories and exports to quiz_summary_report.txt.
